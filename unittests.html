🏆 Level-up challenges🏆 闯关挑战

Mixes sections from one chapter — pass it to unlock the next.把一章的内容混在一起考——通过才能解锁下一关。

10 questions per level (12 for the final). Miss once and you get a hint; miss twice and you get the full solution, and it goes into your review list.每关 10 题（期末 12 题）。错一次会给提示；错两次直接给完整解法，并自动收进复习清单。
