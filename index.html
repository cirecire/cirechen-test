孩子在公立学校读书，之后计划转国际轨道。这条路上有个很实际的问题：数学和物理，往后都得用英文学。

我遇到的难处主要有几个：课本里的概念在英文课堂叫什么、两边讲法不一样、还有用英文一步步推导的能力。想找个老师专门补，得同时懂国内和英美两套体系、又用英文学上课，这样的人不好找，也不便宜。

可汗学院内容不错，但体系跟公立校对不上，孩子脑子里容易装两套对不上的逻辑；视频几千个，自己挑、自己排顺序，大人也难。所以我照课本的目录，给每个知识点配上可汗里对应的那节——白天学校中文学一遍，回家在可汗上把英文那一遍走一遍，两套就接上了。少数教材有、可汗没有的，用图解代替。

这个东西是我给自己孩子搭的，不卖。如果你们家教材版本和孩子情况合适，拿去用就行。

My child is in a Chinese public school, with plans to switch to the international track. One very practical issue on that road: maths and physics will, sooner or later, have to be learned in English.

The hard parts I ran into: what the textbook concepts are called in an English classroom, the different ways the two systems explain things, and the skill of following a proof step by step in English. A tutor who covers all that — knowing both the Chinese and the English systems and teaching in English — is hard to find, and costs accordingly.

Khan Academy has good material, but its structure isn't the public-school structure, so a child ends up holding two logics that don't line up. And with thousands of videos, picking and sequencing a path alone is hard even for an adult. So I used the textbook's contents as the map, and put the matching Khan lesson onto each topic: learned in Chinese at school by day, walked through in English on Khan at home, and the two meet. For the few topics the textbook has but Khan doesn't, I used diagrams instead.

This is something I built for my own child. It isn't for sale. If your textbook edition and your child's situation fit, feel free to use it.

I'm not a teacher, and I don't really write code, so there will be mistakes and gaps — please bear with me. If you spot a problem or have a suggestion, I'd be glad to hear it, and I'll keep improving it.
