💛 About this site — parents, start here💛 关于这个网站 · 家长先看这里

A self-study companion for Grade 8, semester 1 (BNU 2026 edition). Your child works through it alone; you can follow the progress without knowing the maths. 给孩子自学用的八年级上册（北师大版 2026）学习站。孩子自己往下走，家长不用懂数学也能看懂学到哪了。

Who it's for给谁用

A child in Grade 8, semester 1. Use it after class to go over the day's topic, before a unit test to revise, or as a sweep before the exam. Also fine as a holiday catch-up — every chapter opens independently. 八年级上册的孩子。课后过一遍当天的内容，单元考前复习，或者期末前整体扫一遍；假期想补哪章也行，每一章都是独立打开的。

Why it's built this way设计思路

1. Chapter order and the point of each topic follow the BNU textbook, so it lines up with what school is covering right now.
2. Explanations, videos, practice and tests follow the Khan Academy chain. A textbook sometimes jumps straight to a result; Khan spells out the steps in between — those are exactly the steps added here.
3. Watching is not the same as learning: each section needs 5 correct in a row to clear. Mistakes go into a review list and come back a few days later.
4. Videos play locally. No internet needed, and nothing links out to other sites. 1. 章节顺序和每个考点的落点都跟着北师大教材走，和学校正在讲的内容对得上。
2. 讲解、视频、练习、测试走的是可汗学院那套完整链条。教材有时会一句话直接给结论，可汗把中间的每一步都讲出来——补进去的正是这几步。
3. 看过不等于学会：每节要连对 5 题才算过关。做错的进错题本，过几天会再考一次。
4. 视频是本地播放，不用联网，也不会跳去别的网站。

What your child gets out of it孩子能得到什么

• Their own pace: anything they get stuck on can be replayed, with no need to keep up with the class.
• Each section opens with a quick check — material they already know is skipped, so no time is wasted.
• Mastery is visible: you can see which topics were only looked at and which are actually solid.
• English maths vocabulary builds up along the way. If that is a strain, switch the display to bilingual or Chinese-led — it can be changed at any time. • 按自己的节奏来：卡住的地方可以反复看，不用跟着全班的速度。
• 每节开头先测一下，已经会的直接跳过，不浪费时间。
• 掌握度看得见：哪一块只是看过、哪一块是真的会了，一眼能看出来。
• 顺带积累英文数学词汇。觉得吃力就把显示切成中英对照或中文为主，随时可以改。
