💛 给家长的一封信 · 我为什么做了这个网站 💛 A letter to parents — why I built this site

现在很多孩子都在公立学校读书，之后计划转轨国际学校。这条路上有个绕不开的问题：将来，数学是要用英文学的。

这件事难在哪里？通常是三件事。

一是术语。不是单词不认识，而是「一次函数」「方差」「勾股定理」这些概念，在英文课堂里叫什么、怎么用，孩子还没建立直觉。

二是教法。同样是一次函数，英美课堂的切入角度和推导习惯，跟国内不一样。

三是用英语做数学推理的能力。单词懂，但要跟上用英文一步步铺开的论证，是另一回事。

想找个老师专门补这块，得同时满足三个条件：懂国内数学结构、懂国外数学结构、还能用英文上课。这样的人很少，收费也不低。对很多家庭来说，这个问题一直没太好的解法。

有人会说，那用可汗学院啊。可汗是好东西，但它恰恰解决不了这个转轨问题——它的体系和公立校对不上，孩子脑子里会同时装两套打架的逻辑；而且内容太多，让一个孩子自己从几千个视频里挑、再自己搭学习路径，大人都不一定做得到。

我做的事，思路其实很笨也很直接：用公立学校的数学课本做索引和框架，再给每个知识点，配上可汗学院里对应的那一节视频。这样，孩子白天在学校用中文学过的东西，回家能在可汗上找到对应的英文那一节——中文学一遍，英文再走一遍，两套体系就对上了。（极少数教材独有、可汗没有的知识点，就用图解代替。）

我在市面上找了很久，没找到这样的东西，就自己动手做了一个。它不是什么产品，就是我作为一个家长，给准备转轨的孩子搭的一个小工具，免费的，也欢迎你一起用。

关于版权要说明一句：里面的视频内容来自 Khan Academy，按 CC BY-NC-SA 许可使用；这个站点是我个人做的学习辅助，非商业用途，和可汗学院没有任何官方关联。该署名的都署了名。

它具体怎么帮到孩子？每个知识点都配了练习题——题目是随机生成的，连对 5 题算过关；做错的会自动进错题本，过几天再考一次；每一章末尾还有一次自测。孩子英语程度不同，所以我还做了一档中文支持：吃力的时候调高一点，跟上了就调回去。

最后说句实话：我不是计算机出身，不会写代码，也不是数学老师。这个工具一定有不少疏漏和错误。我会一直改，也希望它能实实在在帮到更多正在准备转轨的孩子和家庭。

More and more children are in Chinese public schools with plans to switch to the international track. One thing on that road is unavoidable: sooner or later, maths has to be learned in English.

Where does it get hard? Usually three places.

First, the terminology. Not that the words are unknown, but terms like "linear function", "variance" or "the Pythagorean theorem" — a child hasn't yet built the intuition for what they are called and how they are used in an English classroom.

Second, the teaching. Take linear functions again: an English-language classroom comes at them from a different angle and works them out differently from a Chinese one.

Third, the ability to reason mathematically in English. The words are understood, but following a proof or an argument laid out step by step in English is a different skill.

A tutor to cover all this would need three things at once: to know the Chinese maths structure, to know the overseas one, and to teach in English. Few people do, and the ones who do charge accordingly. For a lot of families, this has never had a good answer.

Someone will say, just use Khan Academy. Khan is wonderful — but it does not actually solve this particular problem. Its structure is not the public-school structure, so a child ends up holding two course logics that fight each other. And it is vast: asking a child to pick the right videos out of thousands and then build their own path is unrealistic — most adults couldn't.

What I built is, honestly, simple and a bit blunt: take the public-school textbook as the index and the framework, then put the matching Khan Academy lesson onto each topic. So the things a child learns in Chinese at school during the day have a corresponding English lesson on Khan at home — learned once in Chinese, walked through again in English, and the two systems line up. (For the few topics unique to the Chinese textbook with no Khan equivalent, I used diagrams instead.)

I looked for something like this for a long time and never found it, so I made it myself. It isn't a product. It's just a small tool I put together as a parent, for children preparing to make the switch — free, and you're welcome to use it.

One note on the licence: the video content comes from Khan Academy and is used under the CC BY-NC-SA licence. This site is a personal study aid, non-commercial, and not officially affiliated with Khan Academy. Credit is given where it is due.

How does it actually help the child? Every topic comes with practice — the questions are generated at random, and five correct in a row clears the topic. Anything wrong goes into a review list that comes back a few days later, and each chapter ends with a self-test. Children's English varies, so I also built a dial for Chinese support: turn it up when it's a struggle, turn it back down when they're ready.

One honest last note: I'm not from a computer background, I can't write code, and I'm not a maths teacher. This tool will have its share of gaps and mistakes. I'll keep working on it, and I hope it genuinely helps more children and families preparing for the same move.
