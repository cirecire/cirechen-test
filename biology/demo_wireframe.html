Step 3 · Notes（核心要点）

  • a² + b² = c² — c is the longest side (hypotenuse), opposite the right angle.
  • The hypotenuse is always the longest side.
  • Common triples: (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17).
  • Use it to find any missing side — or check if a triangle is right-angled.
