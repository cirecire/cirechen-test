💛 关于这个网站 · 家长先看这里 💛 About this site — parents, start here

给计划转轨国际学校、或者准备出国留学的家庭。孩子白天在公立校用北师大版教材学了一遍这些知识，回到家再用这个工具把同样的内容用英文走一遍——适应英文讲法、英文思维和英美教法里的术语，不浪费额外的时间和精力。 For families planning a transition to international school, or preparing to study abroad. Your child already learns this content in Chinese at a public school during the day — this tool walks through the same topics again in English, building familiarity with English instruction, English-language reasoning, and the terminology used in British and American classrooms. No extra time or energy wasted.

解决什么问题 What problem it solves

想让孩子提前适应转轨后的教学，但不知道怎么开始、没有抓手。这个工具正好补上：孩子因为白天在公立校已经用中文听过一遍，再听英文版本就能跟上，不会从零开始。课堂上没完全听懂的推导过程，也能在这里一步步弄清楚。 You want your child to get used to international-school teaching ahead of time, but it's hard to know where to start — there's no ready-made tool. This fills that gap: because your child has already heard the material in Chinese at school, the English version feels familiar, not from scratch. And any derivation or step that didn't fully click in class can be worked through here, step by step.

怎么做到的 How it works

1. 章节顺序和每个考点都跟着北师大教材走，和学校正在讲的内容对得上。同一个知识点，学校讲一遍中文，这里再走一遍英文。
2. 讲解、视频、练习、测试走的是可汗学院那套完整链条。教材一句话直接给结论的地方，可汗把中间每一步都讲出来——孩子能真正弄懂，而不是背下来。
3. 每节要连对 5 题才算过关。做错的进错题本，过几天会再考一次，确保真的会了。
4. 全英文教学，关键词在门厅标注一次中文。觉得吃力可以随时切换中英对照或中文为主。
5. 视频本地播放，不用联网，也不跳去别的网站。 1. Chapter order and every topic follow the BNU textbook, lining up with what school is covering right now. Same knowledge point — once in Chinese at school, once in English here.
2. Explanations, videos, practice and tests follow the Khan Academy chain. Where the textbook jumps straight to a result, Khan spells out every step in between — so your child truly understands, not just memorises.
3. Each section needs 5 correct in a row to clear. Mistakes go into a review list and come back a few days later, making sure it really sticks.
4. All-English instruction, with each key term glossed in Chinese once in the chapter lobby. If it's a strain, switch to bilingual or Chinese-led at any time.
5. Videos play locally. No internet needed, and nothing links out to other sites.

• 课堂上没听懂的推导细节，在这里一步步看清楚、练明白。
• 按自己的节奏来：卡住的地方反复看，不用跟着全班速度。
• 掌握度看得见：哪一块只是看过、哪一块真的会了，一眼能看出来。 • Re-learning already-known content in English — not starting from scratch, so it's efficient.
• Gets used to the pace, style and terminology of an English-language maths classroom, so the transition won't feel alien.
• Any derivation or detail that didn't fully click in class can be seen step by step and practised to mastery here.
• Their own pace: anything they get stuck on can be replayed, with no need to keep up with the class.
